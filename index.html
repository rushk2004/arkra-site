THE ARKRA PLEDGE

No climate penalty.

A home with higher risk should get more help. A verified improvement can unlock a reward.

  • Reward-only pricing

    A rating alone would never be used to decline a mortgage or increase a rate or premium.

  • Open to challenge

    Households can see sources, correct data and withdraw consent for optional sensors.

  • Help on your terms

    Phone, branch and assisted home visits sit alongside the digital journey.

ROADMAP TO 2039

Start at remortgage. Earn the next step.

The pilot tests whether lower claims, lower credit losses and customer retention can fund the proposed reward.

2026–27 · PILOT

Know and fund

2028–30 · REWARD

Test the Dividend

Trial evidence checks and reward economics. Extend the journey to landlords and renters.

2030–34 · PROTECT

Act before damage

Pilot consented Storm Mode, assisted outreach and street schemes with local partners.

Make proof portable
